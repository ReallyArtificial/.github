# Maintainers

Really Artificial is currently run by one human maintainer.

| Who | Role | Scope |
|-----|------|-------|
| [@josharsh](https://github.com/josharsh) (Harsh Joshi) | Lead maintainer | Every repository in the org. Final say on direction, merges, releases, and security. |
| [@fridayjoshi](https://github.com/fridayjoshi) | AI collaborator | Writes code, tests, and first-draft PRs under Harsh's direction. Has no merge rights and does not sign off on anything. |

Final say rests with the lead maintainer until the org has three human maintainers. At that point we adopt the [Minimum Viable Governance](https://github.com/github/MVG/blob/main/project-docs/GOVERNANCE.md) model: maintainers decide by consensus, and add or remove maintainers by approval of the existing ones.

How you get here: after three merged pull requests in a repository you can ask for triage rights on it. Merge rights are by invitation, per repository, and go to people whose issues and PRs have already shown they understand the project. If that is you, say so in an issue.

Contact: open an issue in the relevant repository, or start a thread in [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/ReallyArtificial/discussions).
